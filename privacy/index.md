## English

TapMe.plus is a local number puzzle game. The first release does not require a TapMe.plus account, does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track users. On iPhone and iPad, you may use Apple Game Center leaderboards if you are signed in to Game Center.

- Game scores, preferences, and local progress are stored on your device.
- If Game Center is available and you submit or view leaderboard scores, that leaderboard activity is handled by Apple Game Center under Apple's terms and privacy policy.
- TapMe.plus does not collect personal data from the app.
- TapMe.plus does not sell, share, or transfer personal data.
- TapMe.plus does not use location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone permissions.

If online features beyond Apple Game Center are added later, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact: [support@tapme.plus](mailto:support@tapme.plus)

## 中文

TapMe.plus 是一款本地数字益智游戏。首发版本不需要 TapMe.plus 自建账号，不展示广告，不接入第三方统计，也不会跟踪用户。在 iPhone 和 iPad 上，如果你已登录 Game Center，可以选择使用 Apple Game Center 排行榜。

- 游戏分数、偏好设置和本地进度只保存在你的设备中。
- 如果 Game Center 可用，并且你提交或查看排行榜分数，相关排行榜活动由 Apple Game Center 按 Apple 的条款和隐私政策处理。
- TapMe.plus 不会从 App 中收集个人数据。
- TapMe.plus 不会出售、共享或转移个人数据。
- TapMe.plus 不使用定位、通讯录、照片、相机或麦克风权限。

如果未来加入 Apple Game Center 之外的在线功能，我们会在发布前更新本隐私政策。

联系邮箱：[support@tapme.plus](mailto:support@tapme.plus)
