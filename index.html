One rule, many decisions.

TapMe.plus keeps the surface simple so every move is about target choice, merge position, and whether the next drop can continue the chain.

1

Tap to raise

Pick the tile that should become your merge target. The number increases by one immediately.

2

Merge connected matches

Three or more equal numbers connected horizontally or vertically collapse into the target tile.

3

Plan the chain

After a merge, tiles drop and refill. Strong moves leave the board ready for the next reaction.
